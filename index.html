About this project

Team C.C.A. bridges the gap between traditional chess and modern machine learning through two core projects:

Instant Skill Assessment: Traditional systems take dozens of games to find your rating. We built an XGBoost pipeline that predicts a player's ELO from a single game's move quality. This enables rapid skill calibration, "smurf" detection, and anti-cheat monitoring.

Custom AI Evaluator: We built our own AI opponent. By training a PyTorch CNN on millions of board states (FENs), our model evaluates positions natively and uses a Minimax algorithm to calculate the best future moves.

Together, these projects prove that chess mastery is deeply encoded in the mathematical quality of every move.

Solution

    How it works under the hood

    The ELO Predictor (XGBoost): Instead of calculating ratings based on wins and losses, this AI evaluates how you play. It uses the Stockfish engine to measure mathematical metrics like centipawn loss, blunder rates, and move sharpness. A machine learning model (XGBoost), trained on thousands of human games, then analyzes these patterns to predict your exact rating.

    The Vision & Puzzle AI (PyTorch + Minimax): When you upload a screenshot, a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) "reads" the pixels to digitally map the board. Once mapped, our custom Minimax search algorithm takes over. It calculates thousands of possible future moves—ignoring bad paths to save computation time—to find forced checkmates and winning tactics.
